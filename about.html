关于这间读经室

About this reading room

圣经书阁是一间逐步成长的中英双语读经室，邀请你阅读、聆听、默想，并带着真实的问题与处境来到圣经面前。

Bible Library is a growing bilingual reading room: a place to read, listen, reflect, and bring your real questions and circumstances to Scripture.

经文、导读与内容范围

Scripture, study notes and coverage

传道书和约翰福音专区提供主题导读与经文选段；诗篇专区精选 11 篇。启示录提供全卷 22 章概览，详细内容的完成范围以章节导航为准。圣经地图介绍 66 卷，并不表示本站已经提供全部书卷正文。

Ecclesiastes and John offer thematic guides and selected verses; the Psalms space covers 11 selected Psalms. Revelation has overviews for all 22 chapters; its chapter navigation identifies completed detailed content. The Bible map introduces all 66 books, but this site does not yet provide their complete texts.

经文引用与解释、默想、祷告文字应分别阅读。中文用和合本，英文混用 World English Bible（WEB，公共领域）与部分较早译本：问答、AI 祷告、聆听页、诗篇与《启示录》已采用 WEB；传道书与约翰福音的部分较早内容仍保留 KJV 等旧译本，正逐步统一为 WEB。具体译本以各页标注为准。约翰福音与传道书的聆听页经文为逐字引用的 WEB 选段（缩略处以省略号标示）；各聆听页的导读、默想与祷告均不属于圣经正文。发现引用或解释需要更正时，欢迎反馈；不要把本站导读视为已经经过全面神学审核的定论。

Distinguish quoted Scripture from explanation, reflection and prayer. Chinese uses the Chinese Union Version; English mixes the World English Bible (WEB, public domain) with some earlier translations — the Q&A, AI prayer, listen pages, Psalms and Revelation use WEB, while some earlier Ecclesiastes and John material still uses KJV and is being migrated to WEB. Refer to each page's labels. The John and Ecclesiastes listen pages quote WEB verbatim as selected passages (omissions marked with an ellipsis); on every listen page, the intros, reflections and prayers are not Scripture. Please report quotation or interpretation errors; study notes should not be treated as conclusions that have undergone comprehensive theological review.

当前聆听功能可使用设备合成语音，真人录音仍在制作中。不同设备的声音、后台播放及连续播放效果可能不同。

Listening currently supports device-generated speech while human recordings are in production. Voices, background playback and continuous playback can vary by device.

问答与 AI 祷告

Questions and AI-assisted prayer

普通问答按主题匹配预先编写的经文与默想，不能覆盖所有问题。祷告工具调用外部 AI 服务，根据输入生成回应、相关经文说明、祷告和今日行动。AI 文字可能有误，请查阅经文上下文；生成的文字并不代表神对个人处境的直接启示。

Ordinary questions are matched by topic to prepared Scripture and reflections, with limited coverage. The prayer tool uses an external AI service to prepare a response, Scripture explanation, prayer and a suggested action. AI wording may be mistaken: read the passages in context. Generated text does not represent direct revelation from God about an individual's circumstances.

你的输入与祷告本

Your input and prayer journal

  • 提交 AI 祷告时，输入内容和所选语言经本站服务发送至外部 AI 服务。请避免填写姓名、联系方式、地址或其他能识别个人的信息。
  • 祷告本仅保存在当前浏览器，不自动跨设备同步。清除网站数据、使用其他浏览器或更换设备后，可能无法看到原记录。重要记录请使用“导出备份”，并妥善保存下载文件。
  • “继续为此祷告”会发送本主题、最近一次祷告、最多三条后续记录和此次近况，以生成新的回应。
  • 本站使用 Google Analytics 了解访问情况。外部字体、视频、音乐与链接由各自服务提供商处理访问数据。本站不承诺第三方服务不留存数据。
  • Submitting an AI prayer sends your input and selected language through this site's service to an external AI provider. Leave out names, contact details, addresses and other identifying information.
  • Your journal is saved only in the current browser, without automatic cross-device sync. Clearing site data or changing browsers or devices may make records unavailable. Use “Export backup” for important records and store the downloaded file carefully.
  • “Continue praying for this” sends the topic, latest prayer, up to three follow-up notes and your current update to prepare a new response.
  • The site uses Google Analytics to understand visits. External fonts, videos, music and links are handled by their respective providers. The site does not promise that third-party services retain no data.

阅读不需要创建账号。祷告本由你选择保存；在共享设备上使用时，请留意其他使用者可能访问浏览器中保存的内容。

Reading does not require an account. Saving a prayer is your choice; on a shared device, other users may be able to access content stored in the browser.
