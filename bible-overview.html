Bible Overview圣经地图

66 books, one story — from creation to new creation, all pointing to Jesus. 66 卷,一个故事——从创造到万物更新,全都指向耶稣。

The Bible is not one book but a library of 66 — 39 in the Old Testament (before Jesus) and 27 in the New (about Jesus and His church). They were written over many centuries by many people, yet together they tell a single story: God creating, a world gone wrong, and God’s long rescue that arrives in Jesus Christ. This page is a simple map: the main groups of books, and one line on what each book is about. If you’ve never read the Bible, start here — then step into any book on the shelf. 圣经不是一本书,而是一整套 66 卷的“图书馆”——旧约 39 卷(耶稣以前),新约 27 卷(关于耶稣和祂的教会)。它们由许多人、跨越许多世纪写成,却共同讲述同一个故事:神的创造、世界的堕落,以及神漫长的拯救——最终在耶稣基督里成全。这一页是一张简单的地图:各类书卷的分组,以及每一卷“主要讲什么”的一句话。如果你从未读过圣经,就从这里开始,然后走进书架上任何一卷。
