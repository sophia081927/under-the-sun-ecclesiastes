Revelation启示录

Seeing the victory, comfort, and hope of Jesus in troubled times 在动荡中看见耶稣的得胜、安慰与盼望

Revelation is the last book of the New Testament and the climax of the whole biblical story. It is full of visions, symbols, judgment, conflict, and prophecy — which is why it is often thought of as a hard, even frightening book. But its center is not fear. It is first of all “the revelation of Jesus Christ.” To believers under pressure, suffering, and uncertainty it says: however chaotic the world looks, God still reigns; Jesus finally wins; evil does not get the last word; and God will wipe away tears, death, and pain, and make all things new. So reading Revelation is not mainly about knowing “what will happen” — it is about learning how to live today: watchful, faithful, patient, praying, and following Jesus in hope. 《启示录》是新约圣经的最后一卷书,也是圣经整个故事的高潮与终章。书中充满异象、象征、审判、争战与预言,因此常被认为是一卷难以理解、甚至令人害怕的书。但它的中心并不是恐惧——它首先是“耶稣基督的启示”。它告诉身处压力、苦难和不确定中的信徒:无论世界看起来多么混乱,神仍然掌权;耶稣基督最终得胜;邪恶不会拥有最后的话语;神最终要除去眼泪、死亡和痛苦,并使万物更新。因此,阅读《启示录》不只是为了知道“将来会发生什么”,更是为了学习今天该怎样生活:警醒、忠心、忍耐、祷告,并怀着盼望跟随耶稣。

Humility before the symbols在象征面前存谦卑
Revelation is full of symbols — seven seals, trumpets, and bowls, the beast, 666, the dragon, Babylon, Armageddon, the millennium. This site will not tell you that a symbol “must” stand for a particular modern nation or political figure, or that a certain war “proves” a prophecy has been fulfilled. Where sincere Christians have long understood a passage differently, we say so plainly, briefly note the main views, and then return to what the text says most clearly: God reigns, Christ wins, and believers are called to stay watchful and faithful, waiting in hope. 《启示录》充满象征——七印、七号、七碗、兽、666、龙、巴比伦、哈米吉多顿、千禧年。本站在解释这些内容时,不会武断地告诉你某个象征“一定”代表某个现代国家或政治人物,也不会说某场战争“证明”某个预言已经应验。对于基督徒之间长期存在不同理解的经文,我们会明确说明、简要介绍主要观点,然后回到经文本身最清楚的信息:神掌权、基督得胜,信徒当警醒忠心,怀着盼望等候。
