The question at its heart这卷书的核心之问

Who is Jesus, really? 耶稣到底是谁?

Who is Jesus? Is he merely a teacher, a prophet, or a moral example — or is he God come near to us? And if success, wealth, and wisdom cannot fill the emptiness of life, where is true life actually found? 耶稣到底是谁?祂只是老师、先知、道德榜样,还是神亲自来到人间?如果人生的空不能靠成功、财富和智慧填满,那么生命真正的源头,又在哪里?

John doesn't answer with a lecture. He tells a story — of light entering darkness, water becoming wine, the dead walking out of a tomb — and lets you decide who this could be. 约翰福音不用长篇大论回答。它讲一个故事——光进入黑暗,水变成酒,死人从坟墓里走出来——然后让你自己判断:这位,究竟是谁。

Read the actual opening读一读真正的开篇

"In the beginning was the Word" "太初有道"

John doesn't start with a manger. He starts before time — with "the Word" (Greek logos, rendered 道 in Chinese): the mind and voice behind everything, who "became flesh and dwelt among us." Read the opening lines yourself. 约翰福音不从马槽开始,而是从时间之前开始——从"道"(希腊文 logos,中文译作"道"):万有背后的心意与声音,那位"成了肉身,住在我们中间"的。自己读一读这段开篇。

3All things were made by him; and without him was not any thing made that was made.

The signature of this book这卷书的签名

Seven times, Jesus says "I AM" 耶稣七次说"我是"

Running through John are seven sentences that begin the same way — "I am…". To first-century ears this was startling: "I AM" is the name God gave himself when Moses asked (Exodus 3:14). John is quietly showing you who he thinks Jesus is. 约翰福音里贯穿着七句同样开头的话——"我是……"。在当时的人听来,这很震撼:"我是"正是神在摩西问祂名字时,给自己起的名(出埃及记 3:14)。约翰是在安静地告诉你:他认为耶稣是谁。

Notice what each one offers: bread for hunger, light for darkness, a door for the shut-out, a shepherd for the lost, resurrection for the dying, a way for the disoriented, a vine for the fruitless. Every ache Ecclesiastes names, one of these quietly answers. 留意每一句在回应什么:粮,给饥饿的;光,给黑暗中的;门,给被关在外面的;牧人,给迷失的;复活,给将死的;道路,给迷茫的;葡萄树,给不结果子的。传道书说出的每一种疼痛,这里都有一句在安静地回应。

Book one meets book two第一卷,遇见第二卷

From "under the sun" to "the Light of Life" 从"日光之下"到"生命之光"

Four honest hand-offs四句诚实的接力

Ecclesiastes: we cannot hold eternity by ourselves.  →  John: eternal life comes to us in Christ.《传道书》说,人不能靠自己抓住永恒。 →  《约翰福音》说,永恒的生命在基督里临到人。

Ecclesiastes: death comes to all.  →  John: Jesus is the resurrection and the life.《传道书》说,死亡临到众人。 →  《约翰福音》说,耶稣是复活与生命。

Ecclesiastes: eternity is set in the human heart.  →  John: whoever believes in him has eternal life.《传道书》说,人心里有永恒。 →  《约翰福音》说,信祂的人有永生。

Ecclesiastes: everything under the sun is limited.  →  John: the true Light has come into the world.《传道书》说,日光之下的一切都有限。 →  《约翰福音》说,真光已经来到世界。
