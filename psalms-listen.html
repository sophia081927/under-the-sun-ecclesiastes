Eleven psalms, in Chinese or English. Tap one and your device reads it aloud — right now.十一篇诗,中文或英文。点一篇,设备即刻读给你听。

Current audio uses temporary text-to-speech. A more natural professional female voice recording can be added later.当前为临时语音朗读版本,未来可升级为更自然的专业女声录音。

＋ Want a more natural female voice? (one-time phone setup)＋ 想要更自然的女声?(手机一次性设置)

iPhone: Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → download an enhanced voice (Chinese: Tingting; English: Samantha), then reopen this page.iPhone: 设置 → 辅助功能 → 朗读内容 → 声音 → 下载"增强"版(中文选"婷婷",英文选 Samantha),下载后重新打开本页。

Android: Settings → Language & input → Text-to-speech → choose Google TTS and download the Chinese / English voice packs.安卓: 设置 → 语言和输入 → 文字转语音 → 选 Google 语音,下载中文 / 英文语音包。

English voice is not available on this device. Please try Safari, Chrome, or enable English voices in your device settings.当前设备暂未加载英文语音,请尝试使用 Safari、Chrome,或在系统设置中启用英文语音。
