The Light of Life生命之光 An exploration of the Gospel of John《约翰福音》的一次探索

Fourteen quiet steps — the Word, the Light, the signs, the seven "I AM", the cross, the empty tomb. Read one, or read them all.十四个安静的段落——道、光、神迹、七个「我是」、十架、空坟墓。读一段,或全部读完。

01
The core question核心之问

Who is He?他到底是谁?

Everything in John turns on one question. Not "what should I do?" but "who is this?" A moral example, a gifted teacher, a visionary prophet — or something more?整卷约翰福音都围着一个问题转。不是"我该做什么?",而是"这一位,是谁?"道德榜样、有恩赐的老师、有远见的先知——还是更多?

A moral example?道德榜样?
A gifted teacher?有恩赐的老师?
A visionary prophet?有远见的先知?
Or the Son of God and Savior of the world?还是神的儿子、世界的救主?
Reflect默想

Which of these has been your honest answer so far — and what would change if the last one were true?到目前为止,你心里诚实的答案是哪一个?如果最后一个是真的,会改变什么?

A prayer祷告

God, if you are really here, help me ask this honestly and follow where the answer leads.神啊,如果你真的在这里,帮助我诚实地问,并跟随答案所指的方向。

02
Book one meets book two第一卷,遇见第二卷

The human condition & the divine answer人的困境,与神的答案

Where Ecclesiastes ends its honest search, John begins its answer.传道书诚实的追寻在哪里停下,约翰福音的答案就在哪里开始。

The End终局 · death comes to all.死亡临到众人。
The Effort劳碌 · human labor cannot satisfy the heart.人的劳碌填不满心。

John · the Light of Life约翰福音 · 生命之光

A prayer祷告

Lord, I know the left side well. Show me if the right side is true.主啊,左边那一栏我太熟悉了。求你让我看见,右边是不是真的。

03
The dawn of the Word道的破晓

The Word became flesh道成了肉身

"In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God… The Word became flesh and made his dwelling among us.""太初有道,道与神同在,道就是神……道成了肉身,住在我们中间,充充满满地有恩典有真理。"John 1:1,14 · 约翰福音 1:1,14

God did not watch humanity suffer from afar. He personally stepped into the world — with a body, a name, and tears — full of grace and truth.神没有远远地看着人受苦。他亲自走进这个世界——有身体、有名字、会流泪——满有恩典和真理。

Reflect默想

Does God feel distant to you? What difference would it make if He came close — on purpose?对你来说,神是不是很遥远?如果他是刻意地靠近了呢——会有什么不同?

A prayer祷告

Word made flesh, thank You for not staying far. Come near to me too.道成肉身的主,谢谢你没有停在远处。也求你靠近我。

04
The metaphor of light光的隐喻

"I am the light of the world""我是世界的光"

"I am the light of the world. Whoever follows me will never walk in darkness, but will have the light of life.""我是世界的光。跟从我的,就不在黑暗里走,必要得着生命的光。"John 8:12 · 约翰福音 8:12
Light exposes光,揭露
sin, confusion, emptiness, and the hidden realities of the human heart.罪、混乱、空虚,以及人心里隐藏的真相。
Light directs光,引导
it gives a path, a vision, and the very source of spiritual life.它给出道路、方向,和属灵生命的源头本身。
Reflect默想

Light can feel uncomfortable before it feels like relief. What in you would rather stay in the dark?光在成为安慰之前,常常先让人不适。你里面有什么,宁愿留在黑暗里?

A prayer祷告

Light of the world, shine where I have been afraid to look — and lead me forward.世界的光啊,照进我一直不敢看的地方——并引我往前走。

05
Why this book exists这卷书为何而写

An invitation, not an imposition是邀请,不是强加

"But these are written that you may believe that Jesus is the Messiah, the Son of God, and that by believing you may have life in his name.""但记这些事,要叫你们信耶稣是基督,是神的儿子,并且叫你们信了他,就可以因他的名得生命。"John 20:31 · 约翰福音 20:31

John states his aim plainly: this is not an imposition of rules, but an invitation to belief — written so you may look, weigh, and decide.约翰把目的说得很直白:这不是一套强加的规条,而是一份相信的邀请——写下来,是要你去看、去掂量、去决定。

Reflect默想

Have you experienced faith more as pressure, or as invitation? What would it mean to be simply invited?你经历到的信仰,更多是压力,还是邀请?如果只是"被邀请",那会是什么感觉?

A prayer祷告

Thank You that this is an invitation. Give me the freedom to consider it honestly.谢谢你,这是一份邀请。求你给我诚实思考的自由。

06
The seven signs七个神迹

A roadmap of revelation一张启示的路线图

John records seven "signs" — not tricks, but windows. Each miracle reveals who Jesus is.约翰记下七个"神迹"——不是把戏,而是窗户。每个神迹,都在显明耶稣是谁。

Healing the man at Bethesda医治毕士大池边的人
→
restoring the long-term weak恢复长久软弱的人
Feeding the 5,000喂饱五千人
→
the ultimate provision of life生命最终极的供应
Walking on water在水面上行走
→
absolute victory over fear & chaos胜过恐惧与混乱
Healing the man born blind医治生来瞎眼的
→
leading from darkness into light从黑暗领入光明
Raising Lazarus from the dead叫拉撒路复活
→
total victory over death完全胜过死亡
Reflect默想

Which sign meets the need you feel most right now — joy, healing, provision, courage, sight, or life itself?哪一个神迹,正对着你此刻最深的需要——喜乐、医治、供应、勇气、看见,还是生命本身?

07
The signature of this book这卷书的签名

The seven "I AM" sayings七个「我是」

"I AM" is the name God gave himself (Exodus 3:14). Seven times, Jesus completes the sentence — and each answers a human ache."我是"是神给自己起的名(出埃及记 3:14)。耶稣七次把这句话说完——每一句,都回应一种人的疼痛。

The Bread of Life生命的粮
answers human hunger.回应人的饥饿。
The Light of the World世界的光
answers human darkness.回应人的黑暗。
The Door for the Sheep羊的门
answers the need for safety.回应对安全的需要。
The Good Shepherd好牧人
answers the need for guidance.回应对引导的需要。
The Resurrection & the Life复活与生命
answers the reality of death.回应死亡的现实。
The Way, the Truth & the Life道路、真理、生命
answers spiritual lostness.回应属灵的迷失。
The True Vine真葡萄树
answers spiritual fruitlessness.回应属灵的不结果。
A prayer祷告

Jesus, be the "I AM" my heart most needs today.耶稣,今天就作我心最需要的那个「我是」。

08
Encounters in the light光中的相遇

Four people, four kinds of need四个人,四种需要

Nicodemus — religious & status-driven尼哥底母 — 宗教与地位
Relies on knowledge and position; discovers the necessity of spiritual rebirth.靠知识与地位;却发现必须重生。
The Samaritan woman — ashamed & lonely撒玛利亚妇人 — 羞愧与孤单
Carries stigma and deep thirst; discovers the satisfaction of Living Water.背着污名与深渴;却尝到活水的满足。
The blind man — the broken瞎眼的人 — 残缺者
Trapped in literal and social darkness; discovers sight and deeper vision.困在肉身与社会的黑暗里;却得着看见,与更深的洞见。
Thomas & Peter — doubting & failing多马与彼得 — 怀疑与失败
Crippled by skepticism and shame; discover restoration and grace to follow again.被怀疑与羞愧压住;却得着恢复,和重新跟随的恩典。
Reflect默想

Which of these four sounds most like you right now?此刻的你,最像这四个人中的哪一个?

A prayer祷告

You met each of them where they were. Meet me here, as I am.你在每个人所在之处遇见他们。也求你就在这里,如我本相地遇见我。

09

On the last night在最后一夜

Humility & love谦卑与爱
washing the disciples' feet, and a new command: love one another.为门徒洗脚,并颁下新命令:彼此相爱。
The promise of the Spirit圣灵的应许
the Helper who empowers believers to live out the truth.那位保惠师,赐人力量去活出真理。
The true vine真葡萄树
true discipleship is not striving or self-reliance, but remaining connected to Christ to bear fruit.真正的门徒不是苦撑或自靠,而是住在基督里、连于他而结果子。

Are you trying to bear fruit by striving — or by staying connected? What would "abiding" look like this week?你是想靠"用力"结果子,还是靠"连于他"?这一周,"住在他里面"会是什么样子?

10
The climax of the cross十架的高峰
"It is finished.""成了。"
John 19:30 · 约翰福音 19:30约翰福音 19:30 · John 19:30

The crucifixion is not a failure; it is redemption accomplished. Jesus willingly lays down His life to reconcile sinners to God — absorbing the darkness so we can walk in the light.十字架不是失败,而是救赎的完成。耶稣甘心舍命,使罪人与神和好——他吸尽了黑暗,好叫我们能行在光中。

Reflect默想

"It is finished" — a debt paid, not a task left undone. What have you been trying to finish on your own?"成了"——是债已还清,不是事情没做完。有什么,是你一直想靠自己了结的?

A prayer祷告

Thank You for absorbing what I could never carry. Let me walk in the light You opened.谢谢你担起了我永远背不动的。求你让我行在你所开的光中。

11
The resurrection & the life复活与生命

Death is not the final word死亡,不是最后一句话

The true Lord of Life emerges from the tomb — and He comes looking for people, one at a time.真正的生命之主从坟墓里出来——并且他来寻找人,一个一个地找。

To Mary, in her grief向哀伤的马利亚
He speaks her name.他叫出她的名字。
To the disciples, behind locked doors向门徒,在紧锁的门后
"Peace be with you.""愿你们平安。"
To Thomas, in his doubt向多马,在他的怀疑中
He offers His own scars.他伸出自己的伤痕。
To Peter, to offer restoration向彼得,给他恢复
"Do you love me? … Follow me.""你爱我吗?……你跟从我吧。"
A prayer祷告

Risen Lord, You come to grief, to fear, to doubt, to failure. Come to mine.复活的主,你来到哀伤、恐惧、怀疑、失败之中。也求你来到我这里。

12
The reality of eternal life永生的实际

Life that starts now从现在就开始的生命

"For God so loved the world that he gave his one and only Son, that whoever believes in him shall not perish but have eternal life.""神爱世人,甚至将他的独生子赐给他们,叫一切信他的,不至灭亡,反得永生。"John 3:16 · 约翰福音 3:16
"Now this is eternal life: that they know you, the only true God, and Jesus Christ, whom you have sent.""认识你独一的真神,并且认识你所差来的耶稣基督,这就是永生。"John 17:3 · 约翰福音 17:3

Eternal life starts now. It is not earned by merit, wealth, or religious performance, but received — through knowing God and resting in His grace.永生从现在就开始。它不是靠功德、财富或宗教表现挣来的,而是被领受的——藉着认识神,并安息在他的恩典里。

Reflect默想

If eternal life is a relationship that starts today, not a reward after death — where would you begin?如果永生是一段"今天就开始"的关系,而不是死后的奖赏——你会从哪里开始?

A prayer祷告

I stop trying to earn it. Teach me to know You, and to rest.我不再想着去挣得它了。教我认识你,并且安息。

13
The verdict结论

From darkness into life从黑暗,进入生命

The Gospel of John testifies that Jesus is the Son of God, the Light of Life, and the Savior of the world; and that through belief in Him, we step permanently out of darkness and into eternal life.《约翰福音》见证:耶稣是神的儿子、是生命之光、是世界的救主;并且藉着信他,我们就永远地走出黑暗,进入永生。

The Light has come.真光已经来到。
